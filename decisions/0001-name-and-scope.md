# Decision 0001 — Name and scope

Status: Accepted

## Context

The project needs a concise international name for a framework that designs, organizes, audits, packages, and evolves agentic methodologies.

## Decision

Use **MODA**, expanded as **Methodology Organization, Design & Audit**. MODA is a framework, not a methodology, runtime, or certification body.

## Rationale

The name is pronounceable, distinctive, and maps directly to the framework's central responsibilities. The expansion is entirely in English and supports a coherent family of artifacts such as MODA Spec, MODA Auditor, and MODA Toolkit.

## Alternatives considered

- FDMA — precise but difficult to pronounce and weak as a project name.
- FRAME — technically strong but highly generic in software and search.
- FORMA — pronounceable, but the preferred expansion depended too heavily on Portuguese.
