# Audit Workflow

## 1. Resolve the subject

Pin repository, artifact version, commit, primary profile, scope, and authority. Read the subject's agent instructions before inspecting content.

## 2. Classify before judging

Identify the primary artifact and its components. Do not assume declared labels are correct. Record classification confidence and ambiguity.

## 3. Establish MODA evidence

Pin the exact MODA version and immutable source reference. Validate the target manifest and disclosures before substantive mapping.

## 4. Map controls

For every applicable control, record `satisfied`, `partial`, `missing`, or `not-applicable`. Link authoritative evidence. Require rationale for non-applicable controls.

## 5. Inspect packages and drift

Compare specification, skills, knowledge snapshots, schemas, templates, adapters, examples, and installed locks. Use `current`, `review-required`, `incompatible`, `broken`, or `unknown`.

## 6. Produce findings

Classify findings as critical, major, minor, or observation. Separate evidence from recommendation. Do not edit the subject during an audit unless the user separately authorizes normalization.

## 7. Record the claim

Distinguish declared, mapped, verified, and certified. Identify self-audit explicitly. Preserve the accepted report as immutable history; supersede it with a later audit rather than editing it.
