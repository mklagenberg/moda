# MODA Constitution

These principles are non-negotiable across every MODA profile and version.

## 1. Human intent remains authoritative

Agents may analyze, propose, transform, validate, and execute bounded work. Humans remain accountable for purpose, risk acceptance, external side effects, irreversible decisions, and unresolved ambiguity.

## 2. Human work comes before agent decomposition

Make competent human practice explicit before assigning agency. Define the problem, intended outcome, judgments, inputs, decisions, outputs, and unacceptable outcomes before selecting agents or orchestration patterns.

## 3. Agency is explicit

Every consequential activity must declare whether it is deterministic, agent-reasoned, tool-executed, human-decided, or hybrid. Hidden transfers of authority are non-conforming.

## 4. Evidence outranks confidence

Completion and correctness require observable evidence. Fluent output, model confidence, or an agent's completion claim is not evidence by itself.

## 5. Epistemic boundaries are visible

Facts, source claims, user-provided statements, inferences, hypotheses, opinions, and decisions must not be silently collapsed into one category.

## 6. Context and state are designed

Conversation context, execution state, checkpoints, long-term knowledge, and installed operational guidance are distinct concerns. A chat history is not a durable system of record.

## 7. Repository knowledge is progressively disclosed

Humans, agents, and machines receive separate, linked entry points. Load the smallest sufficient context and keep detailed knowledge in authoritative documents rather than duplicating it across prompts and instructions.

## 8. Portability is architectural

Methodology contracts must remain independent of a specific model or vendor unless the dependency is explicit and isolated in an adapter.

## 9. Safety and recovery are part of design

Define invariants, permissions, stop conditions, retries, rollback or compensation, interruption recovery, and human escalation before deployment in consequential contexts.

## 10. Evolution is explicit and reviewable

Version changes, migrations, upgrades, decisions, knowledge drift, skill drift, and audit findings must be traceable. No tool may silently migrate user-authored methodology content.

## 11. Conformance is evidence-based

Self-declaration, mapping, verification, and external certification are different claims. MODA conformance must identify the framework version, subject version, evidence, exceptions, and auditor.

## 12. Client-zero integrity

MODA must satisfy its own applicable requirements. When experience contradicts a rule, improve the framework, repository contract, validator, template, or skill instead of relying on repeated prompting.
