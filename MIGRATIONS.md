# Migrations

This file records required migration steps between incompatible MAJOR versions of MODA.

## MODA 1.x

No migration exists. Version 1.0.0 is the initial public contract.

Future MAJOR sections must include:

- affected controls and schemas;
- why compatibility is broken;
- preconditions and backups;
- deterministic steps where possible;
- human decisions and risks;
- rollback or compensation guidance;
- post-migration validation.
