# Design Model

MODA separates design into four views.

## Work view

Purpose, human practice, inputs, decisions, outputs, evidence, invariants, and proportionality.

## Agency view

Distribution of authority, orchestration, contracts, tools, human gates, stop conditions, and escalation.

## Operational view

State, context, checkpoints, knowledge, security, recovery, observability, cost, and runtime behavior.

## Product view

Repository contract, packaging, portability, versions, synchronization, conformance, audits, evaluations, and evolution.

The views are not sequential phases. A practical application workflow may visit them iteratively as evidence changes.
