# Disclaimer

MODA is a design and governance framework. It helps people make agentic methodologies explicit, testable, portable, and maintainable. It does not guarantee that a methodology is correct, safe, lawful, suitable for a particular domain, or faithfully executed by a specific model or product.

Conformance to MODA means that required design decisions and evidence are present for a declared profile and version. It is not a claim of domain certification, regulatory approval, security assurance, or outcome quality.

High-impact uses require domain experts, appropriate legal and security review, representative evaluations, operational monitoring, and accountable human approval. A MODA audit does not replace those controls.

Agent behavior remains probabilistic. Deterministic validators can check structure and consistency, but they cannot prove that every methodological judgment is correct.
